Responsive Navbar Demo

How It Works

On large screens: Logo is centered with 2 nav items on each side

On small screens: Logo moves to left and toggle button appears on right

Try resizing your browser to see the responsive effect!

This responsive navbar features a centered logo on large screens with navigation items evenly distributed on both sides. On smaller screens, it transforms into a mobile-friendly layout with a toggle menu.
